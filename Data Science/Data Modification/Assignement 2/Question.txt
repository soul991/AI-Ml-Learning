  {"customer": "Ishaan", "stars": "4.5",   "delivery_time": "35 mins", "city": "Kolkata"}

1. CLEAN
   - Convert stars to numbers.
   - Turn delivery_time into an integer number of minutes.
   - A missing city becomes "Unknown".
   - Deduplicate by customer, keeping the LAST record (not the first).
 
2. INSIGHTS
   - Print the average delivery time.
   - Print the count of customers with stars >= 4.
 
3. RECOMMENDATION

   - "Premium" if stars >= 4, otherwise "Standard".
